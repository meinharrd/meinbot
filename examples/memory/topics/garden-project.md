# Community garden

- The city leased a 400 m² plot on Rua das Flores to the neighbourhood association for 5 years. [D] (as of 2026-09)
- Budget for year one is €3,200, mostly for raised beds and a water connection. [U] (as of 2026-09)
- Sam Rivera coordinates the volunteer schedule. [U]
- Planting of the first beds is planned for March 2027; not started yet. [U] (as of 2026-10)
